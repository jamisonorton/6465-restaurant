## Reflection

For this project we were able to come up with the simplest possible design to be able to achieve the functionality we are looking for. 
Complexity adds difficulty to the workflow and can make it more difficult to carry out our ethical responsibility to keep user data secure. 
We are also ethically responsible to make the user experience as pain free as possible which is a credit to the simplicity of the design.
AI was able to assist with piecing the thoughts together for some design aspects but human validation and verification was necessary to produce the desired outcomes.
